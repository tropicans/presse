## Executive Summary

Milestone v1.7 successfully executed a complete visual design system transformation across the entire `isian` application, transitioning from the legacy deep teal / blue palette to a high-contrast **Monochrome Minimalist (Swiss / Tech Luxury)** aesthetic inspired by Linear and Vercel.

The overhaul harmonized both Light and Dark themes through unified CSS variables, precision 1px hairline borders (`rgba(0,0,0,0.08)` / `rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`), subtle ambient elevation, obsidian / stark white contrast tokens, and restrained functional accents. Every public form touchpoint (cards, step indicators, input pills, signature canvas, `/success` confirmation) and admin touchpoint (navigation tabs, collapsible accordion field cards, outline map, floating sticky toolbar, submissions table, filter badges, and AI summary modals) was refactored without breaking functionality or regressions.

All 9 requirements (`MONO-01` through `MONO-09`) are satisfied, validated across 56 unit/integration tests (Vitest), clean ESLint checks, zero TypeScript errors (`tsc --noEmit`), successful Next.js 16 standalone build, and fully operational Docker container stack (`isian-postgres`, `isian-app`, `isian-worker`) passing automated health checks.
