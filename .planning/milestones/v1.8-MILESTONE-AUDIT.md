## Executive Summary

Milestone v1.8 successfully transformed the visual design of the `isian` application to an **Editorial Minimalist Monochrome (Architectural Luxury & Print Precision)** aesthetic.

This milestone achieved:
1. Classical editorial serif & monospace typography stack via Google Fonts (`Playfair Display`, `Source Serif 4`, `JetBrains Mono`).
2. Absolute monochrome palette with `#000000` (pure black) and `#FFFFFF` (pure white) canvas and surfaces, stripping all residual teal, cyan, and soft accent tints.
3. Strict 0px border-radius architecture across all components, containers, modals, buttons, badges, inputs, and chips (`*, *::before, *::after { border-radius: 0px !important; }`).
4. Total elimination of soft ambient drop shadows in favor of an architectural line hierarchy (1px hairline, 2px medium, 4px heavy, 8px ultra).
5. Subtle layered paper texture with 32px repeating line grid on the body canvas.
6. Complete public form redesign with prominent serif headers, 2px underlined inputs, square radio/checkbox controls with instantaneous binary inversion (<100ms), and framed signature pad.
7. Monospace submission confirmation badge on `/success` screen with 4px heavy divider line.
8. Full administrative suite overhaul across `/admin/forms`, builder editor, step navigation tabs, collapsible fields, and submissions table.
9. Multi-tier verification: 61/61 automated tests passing, clean ESLint, zero TypeScript errors, clean Next.js 16 standalone production build, and healthy Docker Compose multi-container stack (`app`, `worker`, `postgres`).
